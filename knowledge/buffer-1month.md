### AI Drug Discovery — Clinical Proof Arriving

- **173+ AI drugs in clinical trials** as of mid-2026 (up from ~24 in late 2023). 15-20 Phase III readouts expected. First FDA approval projected late 2026-2027 (~60% probability). ([Healthcare Discovery](https://healthcarediscovery.ai/ai-drug-discovery-pipeline-2026-clinical-trials/))

- **Rentosertib** (Insilico Medicine): First end-to-end AI drug with positive Phase IIa efficacy. +98.4 mL FVC improvement in IPF. Published Nature Medicine June 2025. Preparing Phase IIb/III. ([Nature Medicine](https://www.nature.com/nm/))

- **GB-0895** (Generate Biomedicines): First AI-designed antibody in Phase III (anti-TSLP, severe asthma). SOLAIRIA-1/2 trials, ~1,600 patients, first dosed Jan 2026. ([PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/generatebiomedicines-to-initiate-global-phase-3-studies-of-gb-0895-a-long-acting-anti-tslp-antibody-for-severe-asthma-engineered-with-ai-302628234.html))

- **Zasocitinib** (Takeda/Nimbus/Schrödinger): TYK2 inhibitor beat deucravacitinib head-to-head in Phase III psoriasis (June 2026). Origin traces to physics-based free energy perturbation. ([Takeda](https://www.takeda.com/newsroom/newsreleases/2026/zasocitinib-outperforms-deucravacitinib-study/))
