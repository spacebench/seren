## Theses and hypotheses

### The honest gap between AI discovery and clinical proof

AI compresses discovery timelines (30 months vs 6-8 years to candidate). But Phase II/III attrition remains biology-dependent, not compute-dependent. Phase I success is 80-90% (vs ~52% historical), but Phase II stays ~40%. The bottleneck is proving molecules work in humans, not finding them. (Observation from: [Curionic](https://www.curionic.net/2026/07/isomorphic-labs-vs-recursion-vs-insilico-medicine-ai-drug-discovery-2026.html); [Healthcare Discovery](https://healthcarediscovery.ai/ai-drug-discovery-pipeline-2026-clinical-trials/))

**Thesis:** The companies that win won't be the ones with the best generative models — they'll be the ones who close the wet-lab validation gap. The real moat is integrated design-build-test-learn loops, not model architecture.

### Pharma is buying platforms, not molecules

Deals are shifting from single-asset licensing to platform partnerships. Eli Lilly + Isomorphic ($1.7B+), Eli Lilly + Chai, Sanofi + Earendil. Pharma wants AI infrastructure, not one-off compounds. Typical upfront is ~2% of headline biobucks. (Observation from: [DealForma](https://dealforma.com/ai-ml-drug-discovery-and-licensing-rd-ma-ventures-and-ipos-2025-review/); [Vision Life Sciences](https://visionlifesciences.com/insights/ai-drug-discovery-deal-tracker))

**Thesis:** Platform deals de-risk for pharma but create a new problem: how do you value an AI platform before it has clinical proof? The answer is probably revenue-share on generated molecules, not traditional milestones.

### The China pipeline is feeding global pharma

Chinese-originated drugs projected ~40% of new licensing deals. 2025 licensing deal value hit 10-year high of $232B. AI-originated assets from China are next wave — faster, cheaper proof-of-concept feeding global pipelines. (Observation from: [Noah Bio](https://www.noah.bio/insight/903d640b-23ef-4216-ac0b-c487667a87e0))

**Thesis:** Chinese AI biotech companies may become the primary source of clinical-stage assets for Western pharma, creating a new geographic division of labor: Chinese discovery + Western development/commercialization.

### Open models are winning the infrastructure layer

Boltz-1/2 (MIT license), Protenix (Apache 2.0), Biohub ESM (open-source), ColabFold (~1M users). AlphaFold 3 remains closed via server only. The open alternatives are becoming the default for pharma companies. (Observation from: [youngju.dev](https://www.youngju.dev/blog/culture/2026-05-16-ai-biology-drug-discovery-2026-alphafold-3-rosettafold-esm-atlas-boltz-chai-rfdiffusion-isomorphic-recursion-deep-dive.en))

**Thesis:** Structure prediction is commoditizing. Value is migrating to generation (designing new things) and validation (proving they work). The model layer becomes a utility; the wet-lab integration layer becomes the moat.

### Wet-lab validation is the binding constraint

68% of tech executives cite poor data quality/governance as main AI failure reason. Computational predictions outpace experimental confirmation. Context collapse: in vitro binding ≠ in vivo efficacy. (Observation from: [Biotech Intelligence](https://biotech-intelligence.com/ai-driven-drug-discovery-clinical-development-machine-learning-reshaping-pharma-rd-2026/); [Peptide Journal](https://www.peptidejournal.org/research/de-novo-peptide-design-computer-to-clinical-trial))

**Thesis:** The bottleneck isn't compute or algorithms — it's the speed of biological validation. Companies that build automated wet-lab loops (robotic synthesis + high-throughput screening + AI iteration) will have a structural advantage.
